Research

AI agents

Penetration Testing of Agentic AI: A Longitudinal Security Analysis Across Models and Frameworks
Viet K. Nguyen, Mohammad I. Husain
IEEE CNS, 2026
arXiv / code + traces

We attacked a seven-agent system on AutoGen and CrewAI with 13 attacks, first on 2025 models and again on their 2026 successors. The framework mattered about as much as the model. We also found agents that fake a successful result instead of refusing.

An Experimental Evaluation of Multimodal Prompt Injection Attacks on Agentic AI Frameworks
Viet K. Nguyen, Mohammad I. Husain
arXiv, 2026 (under review)
arXiv

Attacks hidden in images (text, overlays, EXIF, QR codes, fake screenshots), tracked from what the agent sees to what it actually does. Over 720 runs, the model mattered far more than the framework.

Agent Trust Infrastructure (ATI): A Federated Identity, Trust, Attestation, and Accountability Architecture for Cross-Vendor Autonomous AI Agents
Viet K. Nguyen, Mohammad I. Husain
In preparation, arXiv soon

Each organization runs its own certificate authority for its agents, and every tool call is checked before it runs. In simulation it stopped 84% of attacks, up from 43%, without hurting normal tasks.

Robots and autonomous vehicles

SoK: A Unified Architectural Framework of Cyber Attacks on Autonomous Cyber-Physical AI Systems
Viet K. Nguyen, Kai C. Xue, Nathan Lee, Mohammad I. Husain
IEEE CPS-Sec Workshop, at IEEE CNS 2026

One 12-layer model that fits cars, drones, humanoids, quadrupeds, ground robots and robot vacuums, so attacks on different platforms can be compared side by side.

Experimental Evaluation of Security Attacks on Self-Driving Car Platforms
Viet K. Nguyen, Nathan Lee, Mohammad Husain
arXiv, 2026 (accepted as a short paper at SECRYPT 2026; withdrawn because I couldn't travel)
arXiv

Five attacks on two small self-driving cars over 150+ trials. Each attack leaves its own pattern in steering, compute time and frame rate, so you can tell which one is happening.

Machine learning for network security

Wavelet-Based IoT Device Fingerprinting
Abdelfattah Amamra, Viet K. Nguyen, Adam Cheung, Sarah Acosta, Thuy Linh Pham
Electronics, 15(4):786, 2026
paper

Identifying IoT devices from their traffic. 99% accuracy across 28 to 105 devices, and 71% even during a DDoS attack.

Fourier Neural Operator-Based IoT Device Fingerprinting
Abdelfattah Amamra, Viet K. Nguyen, Adam Cheung, Sarah Acosta, Thuy Linh Pham
In preparation

A federated version that keeps working when clients see traffic at very different resolutions (0.94 accuracy, where a CNN drops to 0.67).
